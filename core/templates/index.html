Features

Everything your records need, nothing they don't

Built around one idea: your medical history should be complete, portable and entirely under your control.

Unified record timeline

Lab reports, prescriptions and visit notes merged into a single chronological view, uploaded by you or your doctor.

Consent-based sharing

A doctor can only view your locker after you approve their request — and every approval can expire or be revoked.

Verified doctor network

Every doctor account is verified before they can request access to patient records.

Global Health ID

Every patient gets a unique GHID at registration — one portable identity across every provider you see.

Direct document upload

Add scans, PDFs or photos of any medical document straight from your phone or desktop in a few seconds.

Full audit trail

Every login, upload, and consent decision is logged, so you always know exactly who accessed what, and when.

How it works

From sign-up to sharing, in three steps

The same flow whether you're a patient building your locker or a doctor requesting access.

1

Register & verify

Patients get an instant Global Health ID. Doctors submit their registration number and wait for verification.

2

Build your locker

Upload existing records or let an approved doctor add prescriptions and notes directly to your timeline.

3

Grant or revoke consent

Approve a doctor's request for 30-day access, or revoke it at any time — nothing is shared without your say-so.
